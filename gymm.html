05:30

Chapter One — Open

The room, before anyone's touched it.

Doors open at half five. The rig room is still, the plates are racked, and the first members in have the whole floor to themselves. No queue for the bar, no waiting on a bench — just you and a cold room that hasn't warmed up yet.

  • Half-racks, straight bars, full plate wall
  • First 90 minutes: never more than 12 members on the floor
  • Coffee's on before you are

Midday

Chapter Two — Free Weight Wall

Nothing's ever out of reach.

Kettlebells 8kg to 48kg, dumbbells to 60kg, arranged so a set never has to pause to go hunting for the next weight. This is also when most coached sessions run — our floor caps at 45 members so a coach can actually watch your form, not just your attendance.

Evening

Chapter Three — Conditioning Bay

Where the room gets loud.

Post-work hours belong to the conditioning bay — rings, rope climb, sled turf, tyre flips. The light shifts here too, a cooler wash instead of the warm strip everywhere else, because this is the one room built for noise instead of quiet.

  • Rings, climbing rope, sled turf lane
  • Tyre flips on real rubber, not a mat
  • Busiest window: 18:00 – 20:30

22:30

Chapter Four — Close

The last set of the night.

By closing the room has thinned back out to the regulars — the ones who train because the day is finally quiet enough to. We keep the lights at the same warm 2700K they were at 5:30am, so the last set of the night never feels like an afterthought.

Open until 23:00 on weekdays. See the full schedule below.

Between the Chapters — The Method

Trained, not left to it.

Every member starts with a full movement assessment. From there, coaches build a plan around whichever chapter of the day actually fits your schedule — not around whichever machine happens to be free.
