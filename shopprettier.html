Care guide

Keep it prettier,
longer.

Gold vermeil and hand-set stones stay brilliant with a little care — none of it complicated.

◇

Last thing on

Put jewelry on after perfume, lotion and hairspray, never before.

✦

Off before water

Remove before swimming, showering, or washing dishes.

◈

Store it separately

Keep pieces apart in a soft pouch so stones and chains don't scratch.

❋

Wipe, don't scrub

A soft dry cloth after wear keeps the gold vermeil glowing.
