Our story

Built for lingering

Cinnamon started as a single espresso machine and a folding table at the weekend market. We grew slowly, on purpose — every pastry is still mixed and shaped by hand, and every drink is made when you order it, not before.

No rush, no shortcuts. Just a warm room, good coffee, and something sweet worth sitting down for.

— the Cinnamon team
